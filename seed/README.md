# Seed corpus (synthetic)

30 past proposals of a **fictional** agency, used to fill the estimation memory (RAG) and to evaluate it.
Every agency, client, person and number here is invented; every PDF footer says so.

| Path | What |
|---|---|
| `manifest.json` | Ground truth: catalog, per-item drivers, approved hours, train/holdout split. Generated by `tools/gen_manifest.py` (fixed seed). |
| `content/PNN.json` | Spanish prose (client brief, scope, assumptions) written by a delegate model from the manifest. Holds no numbers but the drivers. |
| `pdf/train/` | 24 projects: proposal PDF (indexed into memory) + original client brief PDF. |
| `pdf/holdout/` | 6 projects never indexed: their briefs are the eval inputs, their approved hours the answers. |

Approved hours follow hidden rules over the drivers (page count, products, gateways...) plus noise.
The agency's fixed catalog hours ignore the drivers, so on the holdout the catalog misses by **21.0 h per item (MAE)**.
The RAG estimator must reach **≤ 14.7 h** (30% better) to pass.

Rebuild: `python3 seed/tools/gen_manifest.py && python3 seed/tools/check_content.py && python3 seed/tools/render_pdfs.py`
(the middle step is the judge for the delegate-written prose).
